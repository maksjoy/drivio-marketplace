Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026.

P2PCars.ca is an Alberta-focused marketplace for private vehicle listings. This policy explains the information used to operate the website, why it is used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide when creating or using an account or listing, including email address, display name, optional phone number or Telegram username, vehicle details, description, location, photos, favorites, reports, and communications you choose to send to support. We also process technical and security information such as session identifiers, listing views, basic site events, error events, request metadata, and information needed to detect abuse.

Public listing information

Vehicle details, photos, seller display information, and the contact methods a seller chooses for a listing may be visible to marketplace users. Do not publish information you do not want potential buyers to see.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, publish and manage listings, provide favorites and seller contact features, moderate reports, prevent fraud and abuse, operate security controls, troubleshoot errors, improve marketplace reliability, and respond to account or privacy requests.

Service providers and processing locations

P2PCars.ca uses third-party infrastructure to operate the service. Supabase provides authentication, database, and file storage; Vercel provides web hosting and delivery; and Resend is used for production email infrastructure. These providers may process information in Canada, the United States, or other regions used by their infrastructure, subject to their own contractual and security controls.

Storage and retention

Account information is generally retained while an account is active and as reasonably needed for security, moderation, legal, and operational purposes. A listing marked sold may remain visible with a SOLD label for up to 14 days before automatic cleanup. When a listing is permanently deleted, its database records and associated marketplace photos are scheduled for removal from active storage. Limited security, moderation, backup, or audit records may remain for a longer period where reasonably necessary.

Local storage and analytics

The website uses browser storage to keep an authenticated session and certain local state. P2PCars.ca records limited marketplace activity and error events needed to operate and improve the service. We do not sell personal information or use the marketplace to sell advertising profiles.

Security

We use access controls, database row-level security, restricted administrative functions, transport security, file-access rules, and abuse-prevention measures. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should use a unique password and protect access to their email account.

Your choices and requests

You may edit or remove your own listings through your account where those controls are available. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of account information, email support@p2pcars.ca. We may need to verify that the requester controls the relevant account before acting on a request.

Children

P2PCars.ca is intended for people who can lawfully buy or sell a vehicle and is not designed for children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the marketplace, providers, or legal requirements change. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@p2pcars.ca.
