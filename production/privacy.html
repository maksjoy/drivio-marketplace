Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026.

DRIVIO.ca collects information you provide when you create an account or vehicle listing, such as your name, email address, phone number, Telegram username, listing details and photos. We also store favorites, reports and basic listing-view activity needed to operate and improve the marketplace.

We use this information to provide the marketplace, authenticate users, publish listings, allow buyers to contact sellers, prevent abuse, moderate content and improve the service. Seller contact information you choose to include in a listing may be visible to marketplace users.

Authentication, database and file storage are provided through Supabase. Hosting is provided through Vercel. We do not sell user data.

You may remove your listings from your account. For privacy or account-data requests, use the Contact page.

This policy may be updated as the service changes.
