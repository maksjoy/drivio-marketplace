Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026.

This Telegram edition of P2Pcars uses your Telegram ID to keep your account and listings together when your username changes. We receive your name and public Telegram username from verified Telegram launch data. Your public username is displayed on your listings so buyers can message you. We do not request your email address, phone number or Telegram messages. We also collect the listing details and photos you upload. We also store favorites, reports and basic listing-view activity needed to operate and improve the marketplace.

We use this information to provide the marketplace, authenticate users, publish listings, allow buyers to contact sellers, prevent abuse, moderate content and improve the service. Your public Telegram username and seller name are visible on your listings. Open the Mini App again after changing your Telegram username to update your contact details. Listings are hidden if you remove your username.

Authentication, database and file storage are provided through Supabase. Hosting is provided through Vercel. We do not sell user data.

You may remove your listings from your account. For privacy or account-data requests, use the Contact page.

This policy may be updated as the service changes.
