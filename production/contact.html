Contact & Support

Email: support@p2pcars.ca

Use this address for account access, privacy requests, technical problems, listing moderation, safety concerns, or other marketplace support.

Reporting a listing

For suspicious, misleading, dealer, duplicate, or otherwise inappropriate listings, use the Report listing button on the vehicle listing when possible. This attaches the correct listing context for moderation.

Privacy and account-data requests

Email support@p2pcars.ca with “Privacy request” in the subject. Include the email address used for your P2PCars account and describe the access, correction, or deletion request. Do not send passwords, payment information, government ID, or other unnecessary sensitive information by email.

Safety

P2PCars.ca does not take payment for vehicles and does not inspect, own, broker, or guarantee vehicles. Buyers and sellers should independently verify identity, vehicle ownership, liens, condition, history, and payment before completing a transaction.

P2PCars.ca is an Alberta-focused private vehicle marketplace. Legal information is available in our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
